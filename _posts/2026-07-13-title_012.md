I have written up both the theory and a series of applications of ray_limit to other mixing-sensitive chemistries in a ChemRxiv preprint you can download [here](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/abs/10.26434/chemrxiv.15006522/v1).  The DOI is [here](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15006522/v1).
